GOVERNANCE


Trust, safety and governance built in

• Player PII (email, device ad-id, birth year) is masked at the source. The app, Genie and the model
only ever see masked values.
• Minors and players without consent can't get paid offers. Asserted on every daily run: 0
violations.
• AI drafts can't mention prices to ineligible players. A guardrail blocks any that do, and a human
approves every message.
• Full lineage from raw file to app row in Unity Catalog. The model is versioned (@champion) with
its validation metrics.
• Data-quality expectations drop bad telemetry before it reaches a KPI (e.g. 2,414 negative-minute
rows, 245 null player IDs).
